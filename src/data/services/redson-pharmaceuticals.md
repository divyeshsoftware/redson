## Flagship Manufacturing Division

**Redson Pharmaceuticals** is the cornerstone manufacturing unit of the Redson Group. Operating under strict **WHO-GMP** and **State FDA GMP** standards, our mega-scale manufacturing plants are situated at **2, Mahashakti Industrial Estate, Naroda Road, Ahmedabad - 380025**, strategically located 4 km from Ahmedabad Railway Station and 6 km from Ahmedabad Airport.

![Redson Pharmaceuticals Plant](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## Mega-Scale Manufacturing Infrastructure

Our modern, high-tech manufacturing facilities are equipped with prevailing global standard machinery across all operational departments:

- **Substantial Installed Capacities** – Substantial production lines for all types of Tablets, Capsules, Liquid-Orals, Externals, and Injectables, engineered with provisions for rapid scale-up.
- **Dedicated Quality Assurance Labs** – Chemical, instrumental, microbiological, and physical testing labs ensuring zero-defect quality across raw materials, in-process stages, and finished packaging.
- **In-House Research & Development** – Active R&D cell continuously refining formulation bioavailability, patient compliance, and therapeutic stability.
- **Renowned Technical Expertise** – Backed by qualified pharmaceutical technologists and production specialists.

## Comprehensive Therapeutic Product Lines

### 1. Cardiovascular System & Anti-Hypertensives
- **Beta Blockers:** Metoprolol (25mg, 50mg, 100mg), Atenolol (25mg, 50mg, 100mg), Atenolol + Aspirin (50mg + 100mg), Carvedilol (12.5mg, 25mg).
- **ACE Inhibitors & ARBs:** Captopril (12.5mg, 25mg), Enalapril Maleate (5mg, 10mg), Lisinopril (5mg, 10mg, 20mg), Losartan Potassium (25mg, 50mg).
- **Calcium Channel Blockers & Anti-Anginals:** Amlodipine (5mg, 10mg), Nifedipine (10mg), Glyceryl Trinitrate (2.6mg, 6.4mg), Trimetazidine (20mg).
- **Diuretics:** Frusemide (40mg, 100mg), Telmisartan + Hydrochlorothiazide (40mg + 12.5mg).
- **Anti-Platelet & Anti-Thrombotic:** Aspirin (75mg, 150mg, 500mg), Clopidogrel (75mg), Clopidogrel + Aspirin (75mg + 150mg), Ticlopidine (250mg).
- **Hypolipidaemic (Statins):** Lovastatin (20mg, 40mg), Simvastatin (20mg, 40mg, 80mg), Atorvastatin (20mg, 40mg, 80mg), Atorvastatin + Aspirin (10mg + 75mg).

### 2. Oral Hygiene & Specialized OTC Formulations
- **Antiseptic & Medicated Mouthwashes** – Chlorhexidine formulations, herbal rinses, and breath fresheners.
- **Medicated Toothpastes & Gels** – Potassium nitrate desensitizing formulations, cavity defense, and herbal pastes.
- **Oral Topical Solutions** – Ulcer treatment gels and periodontal anti-inflammatory solutions.

### 3. Liquid Orals & Pediatric Formulations
- Cough expectorants, bronchodilators, antihistaminic syrups, digestive enzymes, and multivitamin drops.

### 4. Veterinary Formulations
- Dedicated line of veterinary injectables, anthelmintics, analgesic injections, and nutritional supplements.
