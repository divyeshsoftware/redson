## Export-Oriented Bulk Formulations & Tender Business

**PAR LABORATORIES** is a dedicated subsidiary of the Redson Group of Industries, specially formed for export-oriented products and high-volume tender business.

### Plant Location & Facilities
Our mega-scale plant is situated at **34, GIDC Gojaria, Gandhinagar-Mehsana Highway, Dist. Mehsana** (55 km from Ahmedabad and 25 km from Gandhinagar). The facility is constructed as per prevailing global standards, equipped with modern high-speed blister packing machinery, and operated under **WHO-GMP** and **State FDA GMP** certification.

![Par Laboratories Plant](/images/ns-img-459.png)

## High-Volume Annual Installed Capacities

- **Tablets:** 500 Million / Year
- **Capsules:** 50 Million / Year
- **Automatic High-Speed Blister Packing:** Integrated cold-form Alu-Alu and PVC blister lines.
