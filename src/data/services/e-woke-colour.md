## Regulated Colours & Industrial Pigments

**E-Woke Colour Inc.** is a leading manufacturer and exporter of regulated colourants, synthetic food dyes, and fine pigments. Professionally managed by skilled chemical technologists, we serve extensive domestic markets across India as well as regulated global export markets.

![Colour Solutions](/images/ns-img-510.png)

## Core Capabilities & Product Lines

- **Pharmaceutical & Food Colours:** High-purity certified colours for tablets, liquid syrups, capsules, confectionery, dairy products, and beverages.
- **Cosmetic Colours & Lakes:** Dermatologically approved lakes and pigments for soaps, lotions, toothpastes, and oral care products.
- **Textile & Leather Dyes:** Acid, Basic, Direct, and Reactive dyes formulated for Cotton, Rayon, Wool, Silk, and Nylon.
- **Polymer & Ink Pigments:** Specialized dispersions for Plastics, Paper, Masterbatches, and Printing Inks.
- **Specialty & High-Value Chemicals:** Tailored chemical synthesis meeting specific customer technical requirements.

## Quality Consistency & Analytical Clearance

Every production batch undergoes strict quality control and continuous testing to ensure consistency:
- Heavy metal screening (zero lead, arsenic, mercury, or prohibited heavy metals).
- High tinctorial strength and batch-to-batch shade reproducibility.
- Full compliance with FSSAI, US FDA, and European colour directives.
