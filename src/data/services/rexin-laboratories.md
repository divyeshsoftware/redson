## Certified Food Colours & Pharmaceutical Chemicals

**REXIN LABORATORIES** produces certified, 100% bacteria-free food colours, blended primary pigments, metallic lake colours, and fine chemical intermediates.

![Rexin Laboratories](/images/ns-img-462.png)

## Colour Product Range

- **Primary Food Colours (85% to 95% Pure):** 100% bacteria-free additives approved by the Food & Drug Corporation of India for confectionery, dairy, ice cream, soft drinks, syrups, and pharmaceutical tablets.
- **Blended Primary Colours:** Formulated from certified primary batches, customized to customer requirements to achieve precise hue, intensity, and shade consistency.
- **Metallic Lake Colours:** High-stability Aluminium and Calcium lakes (offered in 12%, 24%, and 40% dye content). Being insoluble fine powders, they eliminate the need to dissolve, offering unmatched heat and light stability across plastics, can linings, pharmaceuticals, and food packaging.
