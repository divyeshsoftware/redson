## Ultra-Modern Sterile Manufacturing & Research

**Redson Laboratories Pvt. Ltd.** operates an ultra-modern manufacturing facility spread over **25,000 sq. feet** with substantial specialized storage, engineered to international quality standards as per **cGMP** and **GLP** compliance.

The facility features automatic filling, sealing, and labeling lines capable of processing **60,000 vials per day**.

![Sterile Manufacturing](/images/ns-img-464.png)

## Annual Production Capacities

- **Small Volume Injections (5 ml to 50 ml vial):** 10 Million / Year
- **Small Volume Injections (1 ml to 20 ml ampoule):** 25 Million / Year
- **Beta-Lactam Powder Injections:** 25 Million / Year
- **Non Beta-Lactam Powder Injections:** 30 Million / Year
- **Eye & Ear Drops:** 25 Million / Year
- **I.V. Sets:** 10 Million / Year

## Quality Assurance Policy

Quality is the life at REDSON LABORATORIES. Our stringent policy includes:
- Modern, sophisticated laboratory analyzing Raw Materials & Finished Products as per **I.P., B.P., and U.S.P.** standards.
- Pre-shipment inspection by **SGS** or recognized international inspection agencies.
- Manufacturing plant operating under GMP adopting Good Laboratory Practices (GLP).
