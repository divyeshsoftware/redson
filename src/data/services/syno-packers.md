## Multi-Divisional Packaging Solutions

**Syno Packers Pvt. Ltd. (Syno Pack Industries)** is a multi-divisional packaging enterprise established under the dynamic leadership of Chairman **Shri Kalpesh Patel** and Managing Director **Shri Vinay Patel**. 

Our products have earned market leadership and widespread acceptance among leading healthcare, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, food, and surgical companies across **India, the United Kingdom, and the Netherlands**.

![Packaging Solutions](/images/ns-img-501.png)

## Divisions & Manufacturing Technologies

- **Vacuum Forming & Thermoforming** – State-of-the-art automatic thermoforming and vacuum-forming lines.
- **Polymer Sheet Extrusion** – Extrusion and processing of PVC, HIPS, ABS, PP, and PET sheets.
- **Flaking & Coating** – Specialized surface coatings and barrier treatments.
- **CNC In-House Tool Room** – Fully equipped CNC milling workshop for rapid, precision custom mould fabrication.
- **Automated Cutting & Shelling** – High-precision hydraulic die-cutting and auto-shelling machinery.
- **In-House High-Definition Printing** – Direct printing on rigid sheets and transparent packaging boxes.

## Comprehensive Product Range

- **Ampoule & Vial Trays** – Precision thermoformed sterile holding trays for injectables and glass containers.
- **Pharmaceutical Blister Packaging** – Custom-engineered PVC, PVDC, and Alu-Alu blister solutions.
- **Surgical & Electronic Trays** – Cleanroom-compatible rigid plastic trays for sensitive surgical instruments.
- **Transparent Printed Folding Boxes** – High-clarity printed visual packaging for consumer and cosmetic items.
- **3D Poster Advertising & POP Displays** – Embossed 3D promotional displays and merchandising posters.
- **Disposable Food Packaging** – Hygienic food-grade thermoformed takeaway containers and lids.
